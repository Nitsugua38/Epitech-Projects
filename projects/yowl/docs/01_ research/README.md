# 1. Market Analysis & User Research
    Platform Comparison: A breakdown of Pros/Cons comparing established players (LinkedIn) against informal discovery methods (Twitter/TikTok). It highlights the "formality gap" and UI complexities that deter younger users.

    User Survey Results: Raw data from initial user interviews (Leo G, Raphaël Leger, Simon Slack). Key takeaways show a high frustration with repetitive data entry and a strong willingness to use a scroll-based discovery interface.

# 2. Product Objectives
    Defines the "North Star" metrics for the application:

    Speed: Applications in under 30 seconds; 20+ offers browsed in under 5 minutes.

    Discovery: A TikTok-style continuous feed tailored to location, availability, and ethical preferences (e.g., company boycotts).

    Engagement: Achieving social-media-level retention without "fake gamification."

# 3. Constraints & Ethics
    Outlines the technical and legal guardrails for development:

    Verification: Fact-checking all listings to eliminate "bullshit" or scam offers.

    Compliance: Strict adherence to GDPR and labor laws (legal age, work duration).

    Anti-Discrimination: Algorithmic safeguards to prevent bias based on origin, religion, or orientation.
