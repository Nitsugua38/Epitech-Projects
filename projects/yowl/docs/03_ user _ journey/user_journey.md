### Main usage scenario

- The user opens the app on the feed page.
- The user's feed contains relevant and tailored job offers: videos (like on TikTok), static pictures or just text (like a tweet).
- The user scrolls until he finds an offer he likes and click on the "Apply" button.
- The user is presented with a simplified applying process thanks to previously saved CV and cover letter.
- Once the application is complete, they can keep scrolling for more.
- The user can also like, comment and share the offer.

#### Friction points

- The user may find first shown offers irrelevant and close the app.
- Some job offer videos might be misleading or incomplete, confusing the user.
- If applying is too easy, the user might spam applications.

#### Opportunities

- The app could show AI powered micro-explanations on each offer ("Why this job?", "Summary").
- Applications control through nudges based on user's profile and application behavior.
- Some social features such as messaging and Q&A to connect employers and users.
