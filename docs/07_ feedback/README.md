# The "Why" (Problem)
Traditional job boards are too slow and formal for Gen Z. Students waste time on repetitive forms and struggle to find offers that respect their specific schedules or ethics.

# The "What" (Solution)
A scrolling-feed app (TikTok style) that allows users to:

Browse: 20+ relevant offers in 5 minutes.

Apply: In under 30 seconds via a pre-saved profile.

Filter: Based on location, availability, and company values.

# The "How" (Constraints)
The app prioritizes trust and speed:

Verified Content: No "bullshit" or fake offers.

Ethical Guardrails: GDPR compliant, anti-discrimination, and transparent algorithms.

# The "Proof" (User Feedback)
Tested via a Figma prototype with 3 external users:

Verdict: High marks for being modern, intuitive, and sleek.

Improvement: The minimalist white UI is professional but needs more color to feel less "sad" for a young audience.
